How ORACILIER decides

Live decision pipeline: facility telemetry & weather outlook drive real-time predictive cooling optimization.

Forecast

Each day’s temperature is typical climate for that date plus a share of today’s gap from normal (75% remains after one day, fading from there). The error band grows with the gap’s uncertainty.

Choose and check

Strategies are scored on water, energy and cost using your priorities. The pick is repeated at the low and high ends of the error band; if it changes, the day is a close call.
